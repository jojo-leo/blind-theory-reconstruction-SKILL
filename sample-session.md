# Minimal fictional demonstration (not from any real book)

This example demonstrates the **phase boundary**, not a suggested theoretical answer.

## Input

User: “运用盲理论重建skill来处理这本书。” [Uploads a hypothetical social-science monograph]

## Phase 1 — correct behavior

AI:

**Research question:** Why do otherwise similar neighborhoods respond differently to a sudden closure of a shared public space?

**Reported empirical materials:**
- Neighborhood A organized several public meetings after closure.
- Neighborhood B had comparable population size but few recorded meetings.
- Interviews in both settings describe different histories of voluntary associations.
- [Each item would include verifiable page references if this were a real text.]

**Your task:** Based on these observations, propose concepts and an explanatory framework. What mechanism would account for the differences, and what evidence might challenge it?

**Stop here.** Do not introduce the hypothetical author's explanation, terminology or predicted mechanisms.

## Phase 2 — learner contribution

Learner supplies an independently written framework. The assistant does not overwrite it with a more canonical account before evaluation.

## Phase 3 — comparison

The AI reads the actual book's theory section, compares its argument with the preserved learner submission, assesses the five dimensions, and proposes concrete improvements.

No fictional author theory is included in this sample to avoid accidentally teaching a canned answer.
